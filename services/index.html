A clear way of working

Start with the problem. Agree on the scope. Then build.

A useful engagement starts with a shared understanding of the outcome, constraints and what a completed deliverable should do.

  • Requirements and priorities are discussed before implementation.
  • Scope, assumptions and deliverables are agreed before work begins.
  • Progress is reviewed against the agreed requirements.
  • Testing and handover are included in the delivery discussion.
01

Understand

Describe the workflow, users, current tools and the outcome you need.

02

Scope

Agree on the first useful version, technical approach, milestones and acceptance criteria.

03

Build and review

Implement the agreed work and review important decisions and functionality along the way.

04

Verify and hand over

Check the deliverables and document the practical next steps for use or maintenance.
